About

I'm an ICT undergraduate at the Rajarata University of Sri Lanka, drawn to the space where software meets the physical world — cloud systems on one side, IoT hardware on the other. Most days you'll find me deep in C or C++, sketching out a scalable idea, or wiring up a board that reports back to the cloud.

I like problems with real edges: things that run, that people actually use, and that break in interesting enough ways to teach me something when I fix them. Outside of coursework, I spend time exploring cloud architecture, tinkering with hardware, and occasionally building small interactive projects just for fun.
